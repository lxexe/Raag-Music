# Code of conduct

Be considerate of different backgrounds, abilities and levels of experience. Discuss the code and give constructive, specific feedback. Respect privacy.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, publishing personal information and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation.

Report concerns privately to a maintainer through a contact method they have explicitly published on their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing personal or sensitive details. This policy applies to project-managed issues, pull requests and discussions.
